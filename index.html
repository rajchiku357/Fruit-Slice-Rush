HOW TO PLAY

⚔️

Slice With Speed

Swipe your finger or drag the mouse to slice launched fruits. Move swiftly to cut through juicy melons, oranges, pineapples, and strawberries!

🔥

Combos & Multipliers

Slice 2 or more fruits in a single stroke to unleash massive combo bonus points! Combo x3 (+8 pts), Combo x4 (+15 pts), Combo x5+ (+25 pts)!

💣

Dodge Bombs!

Never slice the dark ticking bombs! In Classic Mode, hitting a bomb costs 1 life. In Time Attack, hitting a bomb penalizes your score and time.

⏱️

Game Modes

Classic: 3 lives. Dropped fruits or bombs cost a life. Survive as long as you can!
Time Attack: 60 seconds of pure arcade slicing. Maximize your score!
